# LJT-Homepage

Personal academic homepage of Junteng Liu, built with the [Academic Pages](https://github.com/academicpages/academicpages.github.io) Jekyll template.

## Content

- **About** (`/`): biography, research interests, academic background, research experience, publications, skills and contact information.
- **Publications** (`/publications/`): publications listed by venue.
- **CV** (`/cv/`): education, research experience, skills, honors and publications.

Personal information is configured in `_config.yml`; the homepage content is in `_pages/about.md`, and publication records are in `_publications/`.
